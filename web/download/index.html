GUIDED SETUP

Install with confidence.

Follow the steps below. We use genuine Android and Apple support screenshots where available; your device menus may look slightly different.

01

Download the official APK

Tap Download Android APK. Wait until your browser confirms that the Atlas Academy APK has finished downloading.

APK downloadLook for the completed download in your browser's Downloads list.
02

Open the downloaded file

Open Downloads in your browser or the Files app. Select the Atlas Academy APK to begin installation.

Downloaded fileChoose the APK you just downloaded from this official portal.
03

Allow installation from this source, if asked

If Android displays a permission prompt, tap Settings and allow your browser or file manager to install apps from this source. Return to the installer afterwards. This permission is specific to that source app.

04

Review the security prompt and install

If Google Play Protect offers to scan the app, choose Scan. Only proceed if the APK came directly from this official Atlas Academy portal and no harmful-app warning is shown. If Play Protect identifies it as harmful or dangerous, stop and contact the academy. Never disable Play Protect globally. Then confirm Install.

Security firstDo not install if your device reports that the app is harmful or dangerous.
05

Open The Atlas Academy

When installation finishes, tap Open. You can also find the Atlas Academy icon in your app drawer.

Installation completeOpen the newly installed Atlas Academy app.
06

Sign in with your student account

Use your existing Atlas Academy email address and password. Your subscription and learning access remain linked to the same account.

Welcome to your academySign in with your existing student credentials.
01

Open the Atlas Academy web app in Safari

On your iPhone or iPad, open Safari and tap Open Atlas Academy Web App above. Do not use an in-app browser for this setup.

SafariOpen the Atlas Academy web app in Apple's Safari browser.
02

Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen

Tap Safari's Share button. Scroll through the actions and select Add to Home Screen. If it is missing, scroll down to Edit Actions and add it.

03

Enable Open as Web App

On the Add to Home Screen screen, switch on Open as Web App if the option appears. This makes the website launch in an app-like window.

Open as Web AppEnable this option on the Add to Home Screen screen when available.
04

Tap Add and sign in

Tap Add. The Atlas Academy icon will appear on your Home Screen. Open it and sign in with your existing student account.

The Atlas AcademyYour Home Screen shortcut is ready. Tap it to start learning.
Android security: Android may show different menus on Oppo, Samsung, Xiaomi, Pixel and other devices. Allow installation only for the browser or file manager you used to download the official APK. Never bypass a harmful-app warning.
About iPhone and iPad: This is an installable web app (PWA), not a native App Store IPA. Apple documents the Add to Home Screen and Open as Web App steps in its iPhone User Guide.

SUPPORT & SAFETY

Helpful answers, before you begin.

Keep your account secure and install only official releases from The Atlas Academy.

Will a new APK replace my old app?

Future releases must use the same application ID and persistent signing key. Android can then install a compatible update without uninstalling the existing app.

Why does Android show an unknown-app warning?

Android warns when installing outside an app store. Download only from this official portal and review Play Protect results.

Do iPhone students need an IPA?

No. The current iPhone option is the web app added to the Home Screen through Safari. Native iOS distribution is a separate future project.

Will my student account change?

No. Use your existing Atlas Academy email and password. Subscription access remains managed by the academy.
