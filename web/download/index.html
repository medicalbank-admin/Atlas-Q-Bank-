SUPPORT & SAFETY

Helpful answers, before you begin.

Keep your account secure and install only official releases from The Atlas Academy.

Will a new APK replace my old app?

Future releases must use the same application ID and persistent signing key. Android can then install a compatible update without uninstalling the existing app.

Why does Android show an unknown-app warning?

Android warns when installing outside an app store. Download only from this official portal and review Play Protect results.

Do iPhone students need an IPA?

No. The current iPhone option is the web app added to the Home Screen through Safari. Native iOS distribution is a separate future project.

Will my student account change?

No. Use your existing Atlas Academy email and password. Subscription access remains managed by the academy.
