# Anatomy model attribution

The Atlas Academy Anatomy Lab currently references two separate openly licensed model sources. Keep their credits and licences distinct.

## Whole-body reference models and 147-structure catalogue

Source: [Open Anatomy 3D](https://github.com/MervinR07/open-anatomy-3d)

## Detailed skeletal model: 242 named structures

Source: [OrganUI Skeleton](https://github.com/organui/skeleton-3d), using the BodyParts3D 3.0 dataset.

Model licence: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 2.1 Japan](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en). The GLB is an adaptation and must remain under the same licence when redistributed.

Required credit line:

BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan.

Changes made by the source project: 242 bone, cartilage and intervertebral-disc meshes selected; duplicate STL vertices welded; meshes simplified; coordinates recentered, uniformly scaled and converted from Z-up to Y-up; normals recomputed; illustrative colours applied; STL converted to GLB. The source project notes that some anatomical representations may be missing or incorrect.

The source application's MIT licence does not relicense its model assets. The two model sources above retain their own separate licences.

These models are educational visualisations only. They are not diagnostic devices and must not be used to make clinical decisions.
